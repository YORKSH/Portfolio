## Site

The site rises about 6 m towards the north. Earlier schemes levelled the slope to place a single large volume; this proposal keeps the slope and uses it as the section of the building. The path residents already take from the alley up to the hilltop park becomes the circulation of the library.

## Scheme

The four volumes are staggered, each 1.8 m higher than the last. The steps between them become tiered seating, and each roof becomes a yard for the level above.

<Diagram src="./diagram-massing.png" label="Diagram 01" caption="Massing — slope, terrace, light">
The mass is split along the slope (1), a terrace is formed at each step (2), and skylights above each terrace bring daylight deep into the plan (3).

## Interior

Light from the skylights falls to a different depth on every level. The lower stacks are dark and quiet; the upper reading terraces are bright and open.
